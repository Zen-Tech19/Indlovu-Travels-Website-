Our story

Why we do what we do

Indlovu, the Xhosa and Zulu word for elephant, is a symbol of strength, memory and wisdom. It guides how we work: with respect for the land, for wildlife and for the communities who live alongside them.

Indlovu African Adventures was founded out of a passion for tourism and conservation, and a wish to share the Eastern Cape's wild places, wellness opportunities and living culture with visitors from near and far.

Today we offer guided adventures, retreats and family experiences across the OR Tambo District and Wild Coast, always with the aim of leaving every place better than we found it.

Mission

To create transformative travel experiences that inspire adventure, wellness, cultural appreciation, and environmental stewardship.

Vision

To become the leading sustainable tourism operator within the Eastern Cape.
